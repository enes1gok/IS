## Objectives & Learning Outcomes

### Course Objectives

At the end of this course, the student will learn:
* Basic concepts of management information systems.
* The socio-technical view of information systems in organisations.
* Design, implementation, and application of information systems in organisations.
* Key systems applications and global information systems.

### Course Learning Outcomes

Students who pass the course satisfactorily will be able to:
* Define information systems from a socio-technical point of view.
* Classify types of information systems from different perspectives.
* Solve problems related to ethical issues in information systems.
* Evaluate information systems problems in organisations.
* Compare and contrast enterprise information systems.
* Examine global information systems.

## Course Policies

* **Class Attendance:** Students are expected to attend all classes.
* **Class Participation:** Encouraged in class discussions. While it does not directly count toward the final numeric grade, students benefit most by actively participating.
* **Late Submission of Assignments:** Late assignments incur a penalty of **20% per day**. Extensions without penalty are granted only for documented medical circumstances submitted within one week of the absence (medical notes will not be accepted after the course concludes).
* **Make-up Policy:**
  * **Midterm:** No make-up midterms are administered. If missed due to a documented serious medical emergency or immediate family bereavement, the missed midterm score will be imputed using the Final Exam score.
  * **Final Exam:** Make-up final exams are administered by the Department at an officially scheduled time for valid, documented emergencies.
  * **Assignments:** No make-ups provided for assignments.
* **Final Exam Entrance Conditions (NA Grade Policy):**
  Students will receive an **NA grade** and be prohibited from taking the final exam (and subsequent resit exams) if they meet any of the following conditions:
  1. Miss more than 40% of the lessons.
  2. Fail to complete at least half of the assigned homeworks.
  3. Fail to take any of the examinations.
