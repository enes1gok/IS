## 5. Expectations & Academic Policies

### METU Code of Honor
> "As reliable, responsible and honorable individuals, all members of Middle East Technical University embrace only the success and recognition they deserve, and act with integrity in the use, evaluation and presentation of facts, data and documents."

### Academic Integrity & Violations
Academic integrity requires producing original work and properly citing sources. Infringements subject to disciplinary action include:
* **Plagiarism:** Presenting someone else’s ideas or wording as your own (checked strictly via **Turnitin**).
* **Cheating:** Unauthorized assistance or materials during evaluations.
* **Self-Plagiarism / Double Submission:** Submitting prior coursework for a new assessment without explicit approval and citation.
* **Fabrication:** Citing non-existent sources or creating fraudulent datasets.
* **Impersonation:** Taking an exam on behalf of another or having someone take an exam for you.
* **Unfair Advantage / Fraud:** Submitting false medical reports or falsified documents.

### Generative AI (GPT) Usage Policy
* Generative AI tools are **not entirely prohibited**, but their usage is strictly controlled by individual course requirements.
* Students **must consult their course instructor** before using AI tools for any assignment or project.
* Failure to comply with course-specific AI guidelines constitutes an academic integrity violation.

### Communication & Attendance
* **METU Email:** Official communication relies exclusively on your official METU email account (`@metu.edu.tr`). Check it regularly.
* **ODTUClass:** Course materials, submissions, and announcements are hosted at [odtuclass.metu.edu.tr](https://odtuclass.metu.edu.tr).
* **Class Attendance:** Active participation in lectures is vital regardless of whether recordings are made available.
* **Advisor Collaboration:** Students must maintain regular communication with their academic advisors regarding course selection, thesis topic selection, and academic progress.

## 6. Awards & Funding Opportunities

### A. Academic Performance & Thesis Awards
1. **Course Performance Award:**
   * *Criteria:* Completed 7 courses (21 credits) with no failed courses; Minimum CGPA of **3.50** (MSc Thesis / PhD) or **3.75** (MSc Non-Thesis); Active registration status.
   * *Selection:* Automatic (no application required).
2. **METU Development Foundation Publication Award:**
   * *Eligibility:* Papers published in **Web of Science (WoS) Q1 or Q2** journals during/upon completion of studies with single or joint METU affiliation.
   * *Incentives (2022 baseline):*

     * Q2 Journal: **2,500 TL**

3. **ULAKBİM Publication Incentive (UBYT):**

* **TÜBİTAK 2210-C / 2211-C (Priority Areas MSc/PhD Fellowships):** 12-month (MSc) or 36-month (PhD) stipend support for research in strategic domains (e.g., AI/ML, Cybersecurity, Big Data, IoT, Robotics).

# PART 3: Deficiency Program, Exemptions & Registration Workflow

## 1. Academic Deficiency Program

Students entering without a direct background in Information Systems may be placed in the **Deficiency Program**.

### Core Deficiency Courses
* `IS 503` Database Concepts and Applications *(or CENG 302)*
* `IS 504` Computer Networking for Information Systems *(or CENG 435 / EE 444 / CENG 530)*
* `IS 507` Introduction to Software Engineering *(or CENG 350)*
* `IS 545` Object-Oriented Programming and Data Structures *(or MIN 545)*

### Deficiency Rules & Graduation Criteria
1. **Duration:** Maximum **1 semester** (if $\le 2$ courses assigned) or **2 semesters** (if $> 2$ courses assigned).
2. **Passing Grade Requirements:**
   * Undergraduate-level deficiency courses: Minimum grade **CC**.
   * Graduate-level deficiency courses (`IS 5xx`): Minimum grade **CB**.
   * Minimum Cumulative GPA for Deficiency Completion: **2.50**.
3. **Taking Regular Program Courses:** Deficiency students may take regular program courses concurrently by registering them under **Not Included (NI)** status. Upon successful completion of the deficiency program, these courses can be transferred to the graduate transcript via course substitution.

---

## 2. Course Exemptions & Prerequisites

### Exemption Criteria
* **Deficiency Course Exemptions:** Granted if equivalent courses were taken within the last **10 years** with a minimum grade of **CC** (undergraduate level) or **CB** (graduate level).
* **Core Course Exemptions:** Granted if an equivalent graduate course was completed within the last **10 years** with a minimum grade of **BB**.
  > *Note: Exemption from `IS 502` is only granted if taken previously within the METU IS Department. For every core course exempted, an additional elective course must be taken in its place.*

### Recommended Prerequisites
* `IS 545` $\rightarrow$ prerequisite knowledge for `IS 507`
* `IS 501` & `IS 507` $\rightarrow$ prerequisite knowledge for `IS 502`

# PART 4: Academic Rules, Regulations & Doctoral Exams

## 1. Course Substitutions & Credit Rules
* **NI Course Conversion:** Courses taken with `NI` status during the deficiency program can be substituted into the degree program upon completion of deficiency requirements.
* **Duration Reduction:** For every **3 external graduate courses** substituted, the maximum allowed program duration is reduced by **1 semester** (applies to MSc Thesis and PhD programs).
* **Transfer Restrictions:** Courses already counted toward a previously awarded degree cannot be substituted into a new degree program.

---

## 2. Grading Rules & Academic Standing

### Passing Grades
* **Master's Degree:** Minimum passing grade per credit course is **CC**.
* **Doctoral Degree:** Minimum passing grade per credit course is **CB**.

### Incomplete ('I') Grades
* Incomplete grades must be converted to letter grades before the official university deadline. Unresolved `I` grades automatically revert to `FF` or `U`.

### Thesis Performance ('U' Grades)
* Graduate thesis progress is evaluated every semester as Satisfactory (`S`) or Unsatisfactory (`U`).
* Receiving **two consecutive `U` grades** or **three cumulative `U` grades** results in automatic dismissal from the program.

### Course Failure Limits
* Failed courses must be repeated or substituted. Failure to replace or clear a failed course within **4 semesters** (MSc Thesis / PhD) or **3 semesters** (MSc Non-Thesis) will lead to academic dismissal.

## 4. Doctoral Comprehensive Examination (DCE)

The DCE evaluates whether a Ph.D. candidate possesses the foundational knowledge and research competency necessary for dissertation work.

```
                      ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
                      │    Doctoral Comprehensive Exam (DCE)   │
                      └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                           │
         ┌─────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                                                                   ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────┐                         ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│         Written Exam            │                         │            Oral Exam            │
├─────────────────────────────────┤                         ├─────────────────────────────────┤
│ Covers 4 Subjects:              │                         │ Mandatory for all candidates.   │
│ IS 501, IS 503, IS 504, IS 507  │                         │ Covers all 4 subject areas.     │
│ Grade cutoff: Cumulative >= CB  │                         │ No course exemptions allowed.   │
└─────────────────────────────────┘                         └─────────────────────────────────┘
```

### DCE Timeline & Eligibility
* **Prerequisites:** Successful completion of all required credit courses and seminar (`IS 690`), with a **CGPA $\ge$ 3.00**.
* **Timeline:** Must be taken latest by the **5th semester** (Post-Master's PhD) or **7th semester** (Post-Bachelor's PhD).
* **Schedule:** Held twice yearly (May and November). Applications open 1 month prior. Maximum 2 attempts allowed.

### IS Department DCE Rules
1. **Written Examination:** Covers `IS 501`, `IS 503`, `IS 504`, and `IS 507`.
   * *Written Exemption:* Students who achieved an **AA** grade on their first taking of any of these courses are exempt from that subject's written exam. (Students receiving AA in all four subjects must choose at least one subject to sit for).
   * *Oral Qualification Threshold:* To proceed to the oral exam, the candidate's average score across written exams must be **$\ge$ CB** (e.g., scores of AA, BB, CC, DD qualify; BA, BB, CC, DD do not).
2. **Oral Examination:** Examines overall competency across all four core subjects. No exemptions apply to the oral exam.
3. **Final Pass Criterion:** Overall combined average of written and oral evaluations must be **$\ge$ BB**.
