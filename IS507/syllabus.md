# Course Syllabus: IS 507 Introduction to Software Engineering

**Institution:** Middle East Technical University (METU)  
**Department / Institute:** METU Informatics Institute, Department of Information Systems  
**Course Code:** IS 507  
**Title:** Introduction to Software Engineering  

---

## 🎯 Course Objectives

At the end of this course, the student will:
* Know software development process models.
* Know basic terminology of software engineering.
* Understand modelling notations.
* Know requirements engineering phases and fundamentals.
* Know fundamentals of software project management techniques.

---

## 🎓 Learning Outcomes

At the end of this course, the student will be able to:
* Describe and compare software development process models.
* Choose a software process model based on project and team attributes.
* Define basic terminology of software engineering.
* Draw and interpret system models using software modelling notations.
* Define requirements engineering phases.
* Describe fundamental techniques for requirement phase.
* Apply estimation techniques in project management.
* Define planning activities in project management.
* Gain basic knowledge of software testing and its processes.

## 📋 Course Policies

### Class Attendance
* Attendance is strongly encouraged as some materials are difficult to grasp purely through self-study.
* All quizzes are held **in-class**, making regular presence crucial.

### Late Submission of Assignments
* **Penalty:** **1 point out of 100** is deducted for each full hour past the deadline (e.g., **-24 points** if submitted 24 hours late).

### Make-up Policy (Exams & Assignments)
* **Exams:** Make-up exams are granted **only** to students who miss an exam due to a documented medical condition backed by an official medical report.
* **Resit Exam:** A resit exam will **not** be given.
* **Assignments:** There are **no make-ups** for unsubmitted assignments.

### Academic Integrity & Generative AI Policy
* **Plagiarism:** Plagiarism and copying assignments from other students will not be tolerated.
* **AI Tools (ChatGPT / LLMs):** Students may utilize ChatGPT and other Large Language Model tools when completing assignments. However, these tools must **support—not replace—**the student's own work and conceptual understanding.
* **Defense:** Students must be able to thoroughly explain and defend every submission they make.
* **Collaboration:** All assignments are intended for individual self-study. Discussion of assignment questions and solutions with peers is welcome **only after** submission.
